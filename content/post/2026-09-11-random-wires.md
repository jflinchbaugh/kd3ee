[HamUniverse](https://web.archive.org/web/20260713211840/http://www.hamuniverse.com/)
used to exist and have a great link showing
[ideal lengths for end-fed random (non-resonant) wire antennas](https://web.archive.org/web/20260730060321/http://www.hamuniverse.com/randomwireantennalengths.html).

See all the explanation and evolution on the archived article,
but these are the best lengths (in feet) that I always referenced:
> 29, 35.5, 41, 58, 71, 84, 107, 119, 148, 203, 347, 407, 423

The HamUniverse article is linked from
the [random wires](https://udel.edu/~mm/ham/randomWire/indexOld.html)
page from AB3AP.
